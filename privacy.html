Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026.

Personal Assistant is a private tool used by a single person, its owner, on his own Google account.

  • Data accessed: the owner's own Gmail messages (read access), the ability to send messages to himself, and the ability to apply labels to his own messages.
  • Use: producing a private daily summary and notifications for the owner. Data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.
  • Storage: data is processed and stored only on a private server controlled by the owner. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • Other users: the tool is not offered to anyone else and collects no data about other users.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
